# Pre-submission checklist (Contra)

Work top to bottom. Tick nothing you have not actually seen work. If something
needs a real detail you do not have yet, it stays unticked.

---

## 1. The live site

- [ ] **The site is deployed** and the link loads (Vercel or Netlify — see
      `DEPLOY.md`).
- [ ] **Tested on a real phone**, not just a narrowed desktop window. Check:
      - [ ] no sideways scrolling anywhere,
      - [ ] the menu opens and closes,
      - [ ] the pinned Process section stacks cleanly,
      - [ ] buttons are easy to tap.
- [ ] **All six demos open — both ways:**
      - [ ] inside the **preview modal** (card → modal → demo loads),
      - [ ] in a **new tab** ("Open in new tab").
      - inkflow · hisaab · cadenza · atelier-noor · kora · rep-club
- [ ] The **case study panel** opens for each card and reads correctly.
- [ ] The **contact** `mailto:` opens with your real email.
- [ ] **No console errors** (F12 → Console) on the main page or a demo.

## 2. Contra profile content

- [ ] **Screenshots uploaded** — the six demos (desktop + mobile) straight from
      `screenshots/`. Use the `.jpg` versions if a file is rejected for size.
- [ ] **Case studies uploaded** — one per project, from
      `contra/case-studies.md`. Each says “self-initiated concept project”.
- [ ] **Headline updated** — pick one from `contra/profile-copy.md` (under 12
      words).
- [ ] **Bio updated** — pick one ~60-word option from `contra/profile-copy.md`,
      with every `[FILL IN: …]` replaced by a real answer or removed.
- [ ] **The six projects exist** on your profile and each links to its live demo
      URL.
- [ ] **Profile is complete** — photo, skills, location, and no leftover
      placeholder text.

## 3. Account setup

- [ ] **Wallet set up** so you can be paid (Contra → Settings → Wallet). Finishing
      this before applying means a client can hire you immediately.
- [ ] **Payment method confirmed** (bank / PayPal / etc., whatever Contra offers
      in your region).
- [ ] Double-check your **profile URL** matches what you tell people.

## 4. The 30-second video

- [ ] **Recorded** using `contra/video-script.md` (Version 1 or 2).
- [ ] Audio is clear — no fan, echo or hiss (this is the #1 thing viewers notice).
- [ ] **Uploaded** to your Contra profile.
- [ ] Watched it back once as a stranger: could you tell what you do and see one
      example, within 30 seconds?

## 5. Submit

- [ ] **Application submitted.**
- [ ] You know where to check replies (Contra inbox / email).

## 6. The final “open it as a stranger” test

Do this **last**, in an **incognito / private window** (so nothing is cached and
you are logged out of your tools):

- [ ] Open the live URL cold. Does it make sense with no context?
- [ ] Read only the headline and the first screen — is it obvious that this is a
      **web designer for landing pages and marketing sites**?
- [ ] Click one project. Is it clearly labelled a **concept project**, with no
      invented client or result?
- [ ] Open the case study and the demo. Do the numbers you show match the
      screenshots and the live site? (If you re-measured, everything must agree.)
- [ ] Find the email. Try it.
- [ ] Open it once more on your phone, still logged out.

If every line above is genuinely ticked, you are ready to submit.

---

### Reminder
Nothing on the profile or the site should claim a client, a testimonial, an award,
a revenue figure or years of experience that you cannot back up. The six demos
are **self-initiated concept projects** — say so plainly, everywhere, every time.
