# 30-second intro video — script + shooting plan

Written for reading aloud. Simple spoken English, natural and confident, no hype.
Each version is about **75 words** — roughly 30 seconds at a calm pace.

Timing marks (same for both versions):
- **0–5s — hook**
- **5–12s — who I am**
- **12–22s — what I do + one real example from the portfolio**
- **22–27s — why Contra Labs**
- **27–30s — call to action**

---

## Version 1 — more formal (74 words)

**[0–5s] Hook**
> I design landing pages and marketing sites that have to explain something fast.

**[5–12s] Who I am**
> My name is Ahsan Ghani, a web designer based in Arifwala, Pakistan.

**[12–22s] What I do + example**

**[22–27s] Why Contra Labs**

## Shot list (record on a phone)

**Framing**
- Vertical (9:16) for social, or horizontal if Contra wants it — pick one and
  keep it for the whole clip.
- Head-and-shoulders, chest up. Leave a little headroom; don't crop your
  forehead.
- Lock the phone (tripod, or prop it on books at eye level). No handheld shake.
- Record at your phone's highest resolution; 1080p is plenty.

**Light**
- Sit **facing a window** in daylight. Soft, even light on your face.
- Don't put the window behind you (that makes you a dark silhouette).
- Avoid overhead room lights only — they cast shadows under the eyes.
- Shoot in the morning or late afternoon if direct sun is harsh.

**Background**
- Something plain and tidy: a wall, a bookshelf, a plant. Nothing distracting.
- Keep 1–2 metres between you and the wall so the background is slightly soft.
- Remove anything with a brand logo or personal info.

**Audio (this matters most)**
- Quiet room. Turn off fans, AC and notifications.
- Phone mic is fine if you're close (about 1 metre) and the room is quiet.
- If you can, record audio separately with earphone mic and sync later.
- Do a 10-second test and listen back **before** the real take.

**Eye line**
- Make the phone's **camera lens** your eye line, not the screen.
- Put a small mark (a sticky note) next to the lens and talk to that.

**Second screen for the portfolio (optional)**
- If you want to show a page, put the demo on a laptop, slightly to the side of
  the phone, at eye level.
- Glance at it for the example line only, then back to the lens.
- Keep it bright and zoomed so it reads on video; the Cadenza hero works well.

**Delivery**
- Speak a little slower than conversation. Pause after each timing section.
- Smile on the first word; it changes your whole tone.
- Do 3–4 takes and pick the calmest one. Don't chase perfect.

---

## 5 common mistakes to avoid

1. **Reading word-for-word off the screen.** Your eyes drift and the delivery
   goes flat. Learn the beats, keep the script nearby as a glance-only cue.
2. **Bad audio.** Viewers forgive a plain background; they don't forgive echo,
   hiss or a fan. Fix the room first.
3. **Window behind you.** It looks like a nice idea and makes you a silhouette.
   Always face the light.
4. **Talking too fast to fit 30 seconds.** The script is already ~75 words; if
   you're rushing, cut a phrase rather than speed up.
5. **Hype and vague claims** ("passionate", "10x results", "world-class"). Say
   what you actually do — landing pages and marketing sites — and show one real
   example. Modest and specific lands better.
