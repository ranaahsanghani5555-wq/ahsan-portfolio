The problem

Getting paid is the part nobody designed for.

  • An invoice built in a spreadsheet, exported to PDF, and re-sent in three formats.
  • A payout arrives short and the fee is explained in a bank statement, not upfront.
  • "How much will actually reach me?" becomes a different guess every month.

The approach

One invoice. One payout. Everything labelled.

Hisaab keeps the whole chain in one place: pick a client, add the line items, send. When the money moves, the payout page shows the invoice total, the fee and the amount that reached you — in the same numbers.
