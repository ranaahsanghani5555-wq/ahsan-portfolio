The problem

Most tools finish your sentence. That's the easy part.

  • Autocomplete predicts the next word, not the argument.
  • Rewrites arrive in a house voice that isn't yours.
  • By the time you paste it back, you've lost the thread.

The approach

Inkflow reads the whole draft, then makes it yours.

It keeps a working memory of your tone across a document, so a suggestion at the end knows what you set up at the start. Nothing is sent from your voice. You accept, edit or ignore — one line at a time.

  • Understands document-level intent, not just the cursor line.
  • Learns your phrasing from what you accept.
  • Every change is reviewable before it lands.

Features

A working set, ranked by how often a draft needs it.

01

Rewrite in your own voice

Inkflow keeps a live model of how you write across a document and offers alternatives that could plausibly be yours — not a generic assistant voice.

It is important to note that we have made the decision to…

We decided to…

02

Clarity pass

One command strips hedging, filler and passive constructions without flattening the meaning.

03

Tone dial

Move between direct, warm and formal. The knob is one control, applied consistently, never per-sentence guesswork.

04

Team style guide

Agree on terms once — product names, capitalisation, banned phrases — and Inkflow flags drift before it ships.

  • ✓ "sign in" not "log in"
  • ✓ Sentence case for headings
  • ! Avoid "leverage"

05

Works where you write

A browser editor plus a document overlay for the tools you already use. Nothing new to migrate.

06

Private by default

Drafts are never used to train shared models. Delete a document and it is gone from suggestions too.
