How it works — 01

ONE, TWO, THREE,
DONE.

  1. 01

    Pick one goal.

    Build a habit, get stronger, or just stop ghosting your own schedule. One goal keeps the next step small.

  2. 02

    Get a plan in seconds.

    Four questions in, you have a week of sessions that fit around your day, not the other way around.

  3. 03

    Do a little.

    Short, generic sessions — the goal is a check-in, not a punishment. Consistency is the exercise.

  4. 04

    Keep the streak.

    Every check-in feeds it. Miss a day and it pauses instead of dying, because life happens in different places.

Questions — 06

GOOD QUESTIONS.

Everything people tend to ask first.

Is Rep Club a real app?

No. Rep Club is a self-initiated concept project: an invented brand, an invented product and sample pricing. The app is not available anywhere.

Do I need equipment?

No. Every generated week offers no-gear options — bodyweight, walking and mobility sessions. Equipment is a bonus, never a requirement.

What happens when I miss a day?

The streak pauses rather than resetting. One missed day doesn't undo a month of showing up.

Are the plans safe for anyone?

They're generic and low-risk, but they are sample plans, not medical advice. Talk to a professional before starting anything new.

Can I change my mind later?

Yes. Regenerate the week any time — new goal, more days, another floor. The plan is yours to edit, not a contract.

Is there a free trial?

In this concept, pricing is sample-only ($9 monthly or $72 yearly, both marked “Concept pricing”). There is nothing to sign up for.
